# Contributing

Thank you for wanting to contribute.

## Licence

The Program is licensed under the GNU AGPL v3 only (`AGPL-3.0-only`, see `LICENSE`), with
the additional terms in [ADDITIONAL_TERMS.md](ADDITIONAL_TERMS.md). A contribution is
submitted under the same terms. You keep the copyright in your contribution; there is no
agreement to sign.

## Credit

You are added to `AUTHORS` when your first contribution is merged, with your name written
as you wish and, if you want, your organisation.

## What to submit

- Code you wrote, or code under a licence compatible with the AGPL-3.0 (state the licence
  and where it comes from). No new third-party dependency under the AGPL or the SSPL.
- No personal data, secrets or customer names in code, tests or fixtures.
- Tests with your change (`bin/rails test`).

## Security

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability: report it privately to
contact@tartiflette.dev.
